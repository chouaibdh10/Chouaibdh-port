## Description

**DZ-Fellah** is a web application that helps Algerian producers sell directly to customers.
The platform focuses on:

- Promoting local farming and seasonal products
- Creating direct producer-to-customer relationships
- Offering fair pricing for both sides
- Simplifying delivery logistics

## Features

### Producer features
- Sales dashboard with charts and activity overview
- Store management and customization
- Product management with stock tracking
- Order management workflow
- Producer profile and contact settings

### Customer features
- Product catalog browsing
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Order history tracking
- Customer profile management
